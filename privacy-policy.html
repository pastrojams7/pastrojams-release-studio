Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

1. Introduction

PastroJams Release Studio is a desktop music production and release management application developed by PastroJams.

2. Information Processed

Depending on the features used, the application may process social media account identifiers, usernames, authentication tokens, selected video and audio files, captions, descriptions, release information, and application settings.

Information is used to connect supported social media accounts, authenticate requests, prepare and upload user-selected content, manage music releases, and maintain application functionality.

4. Third-Party Services

The application may integrate with TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, and other services. Information may be transmitted to a selected platform when you connect an account or initiate an upload. Third-party services have their own privacy policies.

5. Storage and Security

Authentication credentials and application data should be stored securely and accessed only as necessary. Temporary media hosting services may be used when required by a platform's upload process. No electronic storage or transmission method is guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information should be retained only as long as necessary to provide the application's functionality or meet applicable legal requirements. Users may disconnect supported accounts and delete locally stored application data through available application controls. Information transmitted to third-party platforms is subject to their retention and deletion policies.

The application is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

This policy may be updated as the application develops. The latest version will be published on this page.
